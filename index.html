The Evening

Three moments, one glow

A gentle flow — no rush, no formalities. Stay for all of it if you can.

6:30 PM

Lakshmi Puja

We begin with prayer — diyas, flowers and a short invocation for prosperity and peace. All are welcome to join or simply witness.

7:30 PM

Sweets & Togetherness

Homemade sweets, savouries and chai under the lights. Meet old friends, make new ones — the courtyard stays lit all evening.

8:30 PM

Sparklers & Dinner

A short, safe sparkler moment for children, followed by a festive family dinner. Fireworks viewed — not fired — from the terrace.
